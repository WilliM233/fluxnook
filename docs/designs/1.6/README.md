Four screens, drawn in the Nookkeeper's seat before the spec (House Rules step 0), onto the pages as built at `dev` `a849086`. The canvas is https://claude.ai/artifact/WgzjCiPNVHoGwrF2g5sQw6 (private); these exports are what the team builds to. Every color, size and word comes from `docs/designs/deep-field/` and the 1.2 to 1.5 screens; the one new piece of furniture is the star (a five-point polygon as an inline SVG, filled in `accent`, outlined in `line-strong` when empty, the left half filled for a half), the second icon on any screen after the grip, kept because "4.5" on its own says less than four stars and a half. The exports were rendered at 1280 wide with the real fonts; the dashed "no cover" and "no poster" boxes stand where covers go, since the fixtures carry none.

**As built** (Ch 1.6 Afterword). Eight places the pages differ from these exports, each settled in the chapter. The Journal heading over an open read whose latest finished read has stars says both, "2 reads · last finished at ★ 4.5 · reading now", where `item-journal.png` reads "2 reads · last finished at ★ 4.5" (census C3, Q1 a), L). A read started and finished on the same day prints that one date, not "9 March 2019 to 9 March 2019" (1.6.1 Q1 b), L). A card's stars are 18px in 24×32 targets, drawn tight beside Edit, with no number beside them; the sheets keep 30px in 44×44 (1.6.2 D1). Started and Finished are native date inputs, so Started shows no "A date, or leave it" (1.6.2 D6). The 12px halves and the Started hint are carried to the first phone Browse (L, at the 1.6 exit Browse). The offer is "Read it?", **Log a read** and **Not now** set apart with no middle dot, as drawn, where section 0's prose has one (1.6.3 D4). When the work has a read or watch open, Log opens Edit this read on it with Done and today picked, so it finishes that one rather than logging a second (1.6.3 N9, L at the exit Browse). On the Journal page, a done read with a Started date and no Finished prints "Done" alone, since the date a state line prints is the one its status names (1.6.3 D6, L at the walk). A `?before=` page with nothing under it prints "Nothing older than this. Back to the newest." under the lede, the second sentence a link to `/journal`; it isn't drawn (1.6.3 D7, L at the walk).

Phone width is not drawn: the screens follow 1.2's rules (the item page goes one column, cover first; a read card's header wraps with the stars under the dates; the Journal page's row goes one column with the work above the body; the sheet fills the width; the stars stay 44×44 targets).
