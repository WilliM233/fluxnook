# Ch 1.3 designs: Open Library search and ISBN lookup

Four screens, drawn in the Nookkeeper's seat before the spec (House Rules step 0). The canvas is https://claude.ai/artifact/CPehVxMcpMH21hfELe4xG8 (private); these exports are what the team builds to. Every color, size and word on them comes from `docs/designs/deep-field/` and the 1.2 screens; nothing new was invented for the look.

| File | Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `search-all.png` | `/search?q=carrie` with All | The Books chip; All fans out to TMDB and Open Library and groups the rows by provider with a quiet uppercase label each ("Films and TV · from TMDB", "Books · from Open Library"). A book row's second line is the author and the edition count, not a summary. A book match line names the catalog's own year and author ("Looks like one you have: Night Shift (1977) by Stephen King, added by hand") because Open Library's year can be wrong. A book with no cover says "no cover". |
| `search-isbn.png` | `/search?q=978-0-307-74366-4` | The same box, in ISBN mode: the chip rail greys out, the label says "That's an ISBN · one edition, from Open Library", and one card shows the edition (cover, title, author, publisher, published, pages, both ISBNs) with a Format select preselected from Open Library's `physical_format` (Paperback when it has none) and a "The work" block carrying the work's catalog status. "Add this edition" imports the work (through the match sheet when it looks like one you have) and the edition. The dashed box lists the other three states of the card: in, new, not found, and the client-side "That isn't a valid ISBN". |
| `match-sheet-book.png` | The match sheet for a book | The 1.2 sheet with a book on it: Open Library side (cover, title, author, first published, edition count, what it brings), catalog side (title, year, form, author, source, shelf, what it keeps), Same / Different / Cancel, and the one-line note that the years disagreeing is Open Library's doing and the match is on title and author. |
| `item-book.png` | `/items/<uuid>` for a book | The 1.2 item page with the book facts (Released, Author, Sources with the Open Library link) and the new Editions section: one row per edition, format in Fraunces, publisher · year · pages, the ISBN in tabular figures, and the Open Library link. The line under it says owning comes with the collection chapter. Connected and On your shelves are unchanged. |

As built (Ch 1.3 Afterword): the match sheet's shelf position was drawn as #3 and is #4 in the fixture; re-exported. The built item page gives Editions the same heading style as Connected and On your shelves (Fraunces), not the small capitals drawn here, because the 1.2 page already draws those two that way and matching the drawing would restyle all three (D7); the drawings' small-cap section labels are the stale part, not the page. The Editions row shows the ISBN plain (`ISBN 9780307743664`), as A10 says, not hyphenated as drawn: a right hyphenation needs the ISBN range table.

Phone width is not drawn: the screens follow 1.2's rules (the card stacks its cover above the facts, the chip rail wraps under the box, the sheet's two sides stack).
