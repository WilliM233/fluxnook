**As built** (Ch 1.7 Afterword). Where the pages differ from these exports, each settled in the chapter. The Own a copy sheet's Format select lists the catalog's printings first ("Paperback · Anchor Books, 2011"; the fixture's five read "Hardcover" alone), then the formats the catalog lacks, and a printing option posts `{ edition }` where a bare format posts `{ item, format }` (A7, named in section 0). A long note wraps Edit and Remove onto their own line (1.7.1). Cancel and Escape do nothing on the Own a copy sheet and on the confirm while a post is out (1.7.2 B1, 1.7.3 B1; the 1.6 journal sheets don't hold yet, a Deferral). An untick asks once when the floppy carries a condition or a note, and reads the copy again before it asks (1.7.2). The item page's "Owned via" line sits under the facts, not under a Series fact, which a fresh load doesn't have (census). The titles read "Ultimate Collection 1" to "5" and the fixture's copies carry no notes, so Collection's third line is absent on them (A13). Shopping's shelf heading carries "<a> of <b> to find" only; the row no "· 2 editions"; the page lists every row with no "and 85 more"; the backups line sits under a shelf's rows; the lede at nothing reads "Nothing to find."; a shelf with nothing to find reads "Everything here is yours.", unless a backup on it is still to buy (1.7.3 D5). The header's Collection isn't lit on its own page; no link has ever been (Deferral). Not drawn and built as specced: the shelf row's "owned" / "via <title>", the ISBN card's "and I own it" and "In your collection" on a held printing (Add lands on the work's page; a card for a printing in the catalog has no Add, census), the nook card's "· 38 owned", the two confirms, Edit this copy.

Phone width is not drawn: the screens follow 1.2's rules (the item page goes one column, cover first; a copy's line wraps under its printing; the series grid is two across and the tick row stays 44px; Collection's and Shopping's rows go one column with the actions under the text; the sheet fills the width).
