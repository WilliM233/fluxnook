# Ch 1.7 designs: copies on the item page, the Own a copy sheet, owned ticks on a series, Collection, Shopping

Five screens, drawn in the Nookkeeper's seat before the spec (House Rules step 0), onto the pages as built at `dev` `557e585`. The canvas is https://claude.ai/artifact/LsDsRCSusZR8JgJinXR4sL (private); these exports are what the team builds to. Every color, size and word comes from `docs/designs/deep-field/` and the 1.2 to 1.6 screens. The one new piece of furniture is the checkbox on a series tile (native, 18px, `accent` when ticked, in a 44px row with its label); the pages add no icon. The exports were rendered at 1280 wide with the real fonts; the dashed "no cover" and "no poster" boxes stand where covers go, since the fixtures carry none. Counts in the drawings are illustrative; section 0 and the census carry the real ones.
