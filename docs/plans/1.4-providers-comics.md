## 0. Spec (Nookkeeper)

**What this chapter delivers.** The third provider, and the one the TMNT sheet was waiting for. Signed in, you search Comic Vine for a comic series from the same box that searches TMDB and Open Library, see which series are already in your catalog or look like a run you entered by hand, and add one: the series and every issue Comic Vine lists for it come in together, covers included. A series has a page of its own now, the run in cover-date order, and an issue's page says where it sits in the run and which collected editions hold it. A run of trades imports as collected editions, and what each one collects is picked by hand from the catalog, since no provider carries that as data. The 366 Mirage comics in the fixture, which have no provider yet, get their Comic Vine details attached rather than duplicated. One key, from one Comic Vine account, on the server.

## 1. Feasibility (Nookkeeper)

- **What exists** (`dev` at `920109b`, read on disk 2026-10-29). Three provider clients of one shape: `src/lib/providers/tmdb.ts`, `openlibrary.ts` (436 lines, the larger because of the ISBN path and the redirect guard) and `errors.ts` (`ProviderNotConfigured`, `ProviderError(status)`); `src/lib/env.ts` lines 41–50 hold the optional server vars, each provider three slots. `src/lib/catalog/import.ts` has `create` / `attach` / `writeChild` / `addFranchise` and the poster cache; `editions.ts` has the 1.3 edition import in one transaction; `match.ts` has `catalogStatuses` with `sameWork(row, held)` taking a comparator per type (`MatchType = "film" | "tv" | "book"`, line 33; `MatchRow.source` a union of two, line 37) and a `Candidate` shape with `shelf` and `shelfPosition` the sheet reads; `normalize.ts` has `normalizeTitle` and `normalizeName`. `src/app/api/v1/items/import/schema.ts` is a discriminated union on `source` with two members.
