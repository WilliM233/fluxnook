## 1. Feasibility (Nookkeeper)

- **What exists** (`dev` at `ae357da`, read on disk 2026-11-05). Four sheets carry four holds: `collection/own-copy-sheet.tsx` 89–129 has the full one (`busy` at 89, `typed` at 92, `onOpenChange` at 128–129 gated on both, Cancel `disabled={busy}` at 261, submit at 266; 1.7.3 B1); `shelves/confirm-sheet.tsx` 38–89 has it for a confirm (`busy` 38, `onOpenChange` 58 gated on `busy`, both buttons `disabled={busy}` 80 and 89; 1.7.2 B1); `journal/log-read-sheet.tsx` 118–164 has `busy` (118) and `typed` (122) but `onOpenChange` at 164 gates on `typed` alone, and Cancel at 283 isn't disabled; `journal/entry-sheet.tsx` 68–105 the same shape (`busy` 68, `typed` 77, `onOpenChange` 105 on `typed` or `gone`, Cancel 168). The frame is `src/components/ui/dialog.tsx` (base-ui, 158 lines), whose `onOpenChange` is where every close passes, so the hold can sit there as two props (`busy`, `typed`) or beside it as a hook the four call; the Cartographer picks. Other sheets that post, for the census to confirm: `shelves/name-sheet.tsx`, `shelves/[id]/add-works.tsx`, `search/match-sheet.tsx`, `search/series-sheet.tsx`; 1.5.2 and 1.4.2 tested their posts with `page.route` but not a Cancel during one
- **Nightly.** `.github/workflows/nightly.yml`: schedule `17 4 * * *` and `workflow_dispatch`, a matrix of chromium, firefox, webkit on `npm run build` then `npm run test:e2e -- --project=<browser>` with `PLAYWRIGHT_ALL_BROWSERS=1`; the report uploads on failure. `playwright.config.ts` 29–35 lists the three projects behind that env. The reds the Binder read at the 1.7 Afterword: Firefox `journal-sheets.spec.ts` 86 at `557e585` and `ec9ca3b`, Firefox `series-ticks.spec.ts` 301 at `ec9ca3b`. The star test clicks a half of a 30px star (`star-control.tsx`; 1.6.2) by position; Firefox's hit-testing on an inline SVG inside a button, or its `clientX` rounding, is the likely difference, and that is a user-facing bug if a Firefox click can't land a half. The series-ticks line reads a heading after a tick; a Firefox timing on `aria-busy` is the likely shape. Neither is named yet; A2 names them from the artifacts, which the Binder downloads with `gh run download`
- **Risk, low.** One shared piece touching seven sheets is the widest edit; each sheet's own tests stand as the regression net, and A4's one-mutant-many-reds is the proof the piece is shared. A2 could find a Firefox-only rendering difference in the star that needs a layout change; if so it's a step with its own observable, not a design talk, since the drawing doesn't move. The likely cut: one Signature, 1.8.1 `close-out`, four steps: the shared hold and the seven mounts; the two Firefox reds; the Nightly dispatch; the script and docs
