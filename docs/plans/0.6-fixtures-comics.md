## 0. Spec (Nookkeeper)

**What this chapter delivers.** L's TMNT Mirage tab, exported from the Google Sheet, becomes the third fixture: every row a comic item, the five Ultimate Collections as collected works with an edition each and a copy of each (so "owned via" has real data under it), the backup stories contained-in their hosts, the 26 read rows as occurrences, and the tab itself as a shelf with the sheet's sections. The last Volume 0 chapter, and the one the collection half of the core model was drawn for.

**Acceptance.**
- A1 `supabase/seed/raw/google-sheets/tmnt-mirage.csv` is L's export of the Mirage tab (2026-10-23), unedited, with its row in `supabase/seed/raw/README.md`. The other four tabs are not in the repo
- A2 `npm run fixtures:build` writes `supabase/seed/fixtures/tmnt-mirage.json` from it through a third builder; `king-films.json` and `king-books.json` are byte-for-byte unchanged
- A3 `src/fixtures/shape.ts` gains the `comic` variant and the collection pieces. `comic`: `form` (`issue`, `one-shot`, `backup`, `collected`, `anthology`), `series` (string or null), `series_kind` (`ongoing`, `micro-series`, `mini-series`, `crossover`, `tie-in`, `minor`, or null), `number` (int or null), `year` (int or null, the one variant where null is allowed), `month` (1–12, optional), `source_date` (the cell, verbatim). Top-level `editions` (`{ id, item, format, contents }`, format one of `floppy`, `tpb`, `hardcover`, `omnibus`), `copies` (`{ edition, note }`), `occurrences` (`{ item, status }`, status `done`), and shelves may carry `sections` (`{ name, entries }`) in place of a flat `entries`. Cross-file rules extend to edition contents, copies and occurrences
- A4 Items: one per data row (326), plus one `anthology` per distinct backup host the sheet doesn't already list as an issue (35), plus the five Ultimate Collections as `collected` (366). Section rules, declared in section 1, decide `form`, `series`, `series_kind`, `number` and title per section; no cell is corrected. Ids `manual:…`, unique in the file, the scheme the Cartographer's; the two "Savage Dragon #2" crossover rows are two items
- A5 Editions and copies: five editions, one per Ultimate Collection, `format: hardcover`, `contents` = the ids of the rows whose Format cell names it (8, 7, 7, 8, 7; 37 in all), each owned by one copy. No `contained-in` from an issue to an Ultimate Collection: ownership of those 37 issues is derived through edition contents, which is the Roadmap's "owned via"
- A6 Relations: 47 `contained-in`, one per backup, `to` the Vol 1 issue when the host cell is exactly `Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles #<n>` and that issue is in the sheet (two: #7, #40), otherwise `to` the host's `anthology` item. Nothing else
- A7 Occurrences: 26, one per `Read = TRUE` row, `status: done`, no dates. Read is never derived from a copy
- A8 One shelf, "TMNT, the Mirage run", 13 sections named as the tab names them (Volume prefixed: "Volume 1 · Main Series"), 326 entries in sheet order, the data rows only; anthology hosts and the Ultimate Collections are not on it. `date` is the export date, `url` the sheet's, `description` one sentence saying it mirrors the tab
- A9 `gaps`: one line per row with no readable year (47 blank cells), and one per row a section rule can't read. The Binder's section 5 states the count; the Browse checks it
- A10 `npm test` red on: a cell edited in the raw CSV without a rebuild; an edition `contents` id no file defines; a copy pointing at an edition that isn't there; an occurrence on an id no file defines. Each seen red once
- A11 README's Fixtures section names the third file, editions, copies, occurrences and shelf sections. STACK row only if a package is added

**Calls this chapter depends on.** Roadmap 2026-10-23 (this chapter): comic forms in collectors' words; the Ultimate Collections are collected works with an edition and a copy, not more contained-in; backups contained-in their host, hosts the sheet doesn't list become anthologies; read flags are occurrences; a tab is a run and a run is a shelf. Roadmap core model: Edition is a child of a media item with contents; Copy points at an edition; ownership of an issue is derived through contents and shown "owned via"; Shelf has one level of sections; read is computed from occurrences, never from the collection. Roadmap 2026-10-22: raw exports as they are; only the Mirage tab is committed.

## 1. Feasibility (Nookkeeper)

- **The export, measured** (L's file; byte-identical to a direct download of the tab, md5 `2b024683…`). 355 rows, header `Read, Issue, Name, Publish Date, Owned, Format`, six columns on every row, CRLF, no BOM, three non-ASCII characters in titles (`é`, `’`, `•`; the slug rule from Ch 0.5 handles the first two, the bullet drops to a hyphen). Row 2 is the tab title `Mirage Run`; 13 section header rows follow, `Volume n` in `Read` on the first section of each volume and blank after, the section name in `Issue`; a blank row before each header. 326 data rows (`Read` is `TRUE` or `FALSE`), 26 read, 37 owned; `Owned = TRUE` if and only if `Format` is non-empty, and every non-empty Format is `Ultimate Collection 1` to `5` (8, 7, 7, 8, 7).
- **Section rules.** The columns change meaning by section; the builder needs one rule per section name, keyed on the header text, and must stop on a header it doesn't know. As measured:
  - *Main Series* (Vol 1: 62, Vol 2: 13, Vol 4: 32), *Tales of the TMNT* (7), *Tales of the TMNT 2* (70), *Image Series* (23): `Issue` = `#n`, `Name` = title, `Publish Date` = date. `form: issue`, `series_kind: ongoing`, `series` from the section and volume ("Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (Mirage) Vol. 1", "Tales of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles", …; the Cartographer writes the table).
  - *Micro Series* (5): `Issue` = a turtle's name, `Name` = the story title. `form: one-shot`, `series_kind: micro-series`, `number: null`, title `<Issue>: <Name>` ("Raphael: Me, Myself and I").
  - *Mini Series* (Vol 3: 4, Vol 4: 16): `Issue` = `#n` restarting per mini; `Name` carries the mini's name with its number or part ("Bodycount #3", "Tales of Leonardo: Blind Sight #1", "Michelangelo: The Third Kind, part 1"). `series` = `Name` with a trailing ` #<n>` or `, part <n>` removed; `number` from `Issue`; `form: issue`, `series_kind: mini-series`. Four Vol 4 minis of four issues each, so ids must carry the series, not just the number.
  - *Short Stories* (47): `Issue` = the story's title, `Name` = where it appeared, `Publish Date` = date. `form: backup`, `number: null`, `series: null`, a `contained-in` to the host (A6). Hosts: 37 distinct; `Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles #7` and `#40` resolve to Vol 1 Main; the other 35 become `anthology` items, title = the cell, year from the backup's date cell when the host has none of its own, deduped by exact cell text.
  - *Minor Titles* (Vol 1: 20, Vol 2: 9) and *Movie tie-ins* (9): slid left: `Issue` = title (often with `#n` inside), `Name` = date, `Publish Date` empty. `number` = a trailing `#<n>` in the title if present (then `form: issue`, `series` = the title before it), else `number: null`, `form: one-shot`, `series: null`. `series_kind: minor` or `tie-in`. Date from `Name`.
  - *Supplemental/Crossover* (9): `Issue` = the host comic ("Savage Dragon #2", "Gen 13(Vol.2) #13B"), `Name` empty or a stray "#22", no date. Same title/number rule as Minor Titles; `series_kind: crossover`; `year: null` and a gap each; the stray "#22" goes to `source_note`. "Savage Dragon #2" is two rows and two items; the id scheme disambiguates (a position suffix is the plain choice).
- **Dates.** Five shapes: `Month, YYYY` (196), `Month YYYY` (71), `YYYY` (4), `Month D, YYYY` (6), and two with trailing artifacts (`October, 2010 (online) 1`, `May, 2014 (online) [1]`); `Summer 1993` once. Rule: `year` is the first four-digit number in the cell; `month` is a leading month name when there is one; the cell is kept verbatim in `source_date`; a cell with no four-digit number (47 blank, 0 others) gives `year: null` and a `gaps` line. Nothing is stripped from the cell.
- **Reprints as hosts.** Nine hosts are later printings of listed issues (`Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles #1 (5th print)`, `Raphael (2nd print)`, two `Tales … TPB` printings). The edition model is exactly what they are, and the temptation is to resolve them. Not this chapter: the as-is rule, and the resolution needs Metron's printing data (Volume 1 Ch 4) to be right. They are anthologies today; Deferral below.
- **The shape.** `year` nullable only on `comic`; the film and book variants keep `int`. `editions[].item` must name a `comic` with `form: collected` (a Rummage candidate, like the endpoint-type rule from Ch 0.5). `copies[].edition` must resolve. `occurrences[].item` must resolve. A shelf has `entries` or `sections`, never both; positions increase within a section; cross-file id checks read sections. `providers` enum unchanged (no comics provider yet).
- **Ids.** `manual:` again. Suggested scheme, the Cartographer's to settle: `manual:tmnt-mirage-<series-slug>-<n>` where there is a number, `manual:tmnt-mirage-<section-slug>-<title-slug>` otherwise, `-r<row>` only on a collision. Anthologies `manual:tmnt-mirage-host-<slug>`; Ultimate Collections `manual:tmnt-ultimate-collection-<n>`; editions `edition:<item id without prefix>-hardcover`, copies need no id. All must survive to Volume 1 Ch 4.
- **Counts the Binder should land on** (from the measurement; the build is the proof): 366 items, 5 editions with 37 contents, 5 copies, 47 contained-in, 26 occurrences, 1 shelf with 13 sections and 326 entries, 47 gaps from blank dates (the 9 crossover rows among them). If the builder's counts differ, the section rules are where to look, and section 5 says why.
- **Build order.** The third `SOURCES` line; Ch 0.5's Deferral on half-written sets (a builder that throws after the first file is written) is now live and the Cartographer decides whether to build all before writing any.
- **Tests.** `tests/unit/fixtures.test.ts` extends its cross-file checks to the new arrays; a `fixtures-tmnt-mirage.test.ts` holds the counts and the named facts in the Browse. `exportOf()` and `sample()` reach their fourth copy, so the Ch 0.5 helper Deferral fires: `tests/unit/fixtures/helpers.ts`.
- **Risk, moderate.** The per-section rules are the chapter. A sheet is hand-made and the 13 rules are fitted to this one; a new tab (Archie) will need its own. That's the Volume 2 CSV import's problem, and this chapter is the data it will be designed against.
