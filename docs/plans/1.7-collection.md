## 0. Spec (Nookkeeper)

**What this chapter delivers.** The collection arrives. A copy is a thing you hold: one printing of a work in one format, with its condition, the day you got it and a note. You own one from a work's page (any type: a film on disc as much as a book), tick issues on a series page, or say "and I own it" when an ISBN finds the book in your hand. Owning a collected edition owns what it collects, shown as "owned via" on the issues. A Collection page lists what you hold; a Shopping view lists what your shelves ask for and your copies don't answer. The fixture's five Ultimate Collection hardcovers are the first copies, and 38 Mirage comics are yours through them on the first load. One migration (the condition vocabulary), no provider. The two test Deferrals that fired in 1.6 land first, before a line of the chapter's own.
