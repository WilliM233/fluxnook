# 0.5: The King, on the shelf

Still nothing new on screen. The books behind the King films now exist as data, and they know which film came from which one.

- The Stephen King list's notes are read into 27 books: seven novels, The Mist, The Dark Tower, The Running Man (under Richard Bachman's name), 16 short stories, and Night Shift, the collection they sit in.
- Every film points at the work it was adapted from, 62 links in all. Chapelwaite and Cat's Eye point at two. Twelve films point at Children of the Corn.
- Each short story is tied to Night Shift.
- One shelf lists the 11 works in the order the list gives them: by year, then by which film came first.
- The Long Walk has no note, so it has no book. It is written down as a gap, not guessed at.

**Under the floorboards.** `npm run fixtures:build` now writes `supabase/seed/fixtures/king-books.json` as well, from the same raw CSV, through a second builder (`scripts/fixtures/king-works.mjs`, on top of the Letterboxd one). `king-films.json` is byte for byte unchanged. `src/fixtures/shape.ts` gains a `book` item (`form`, `author`) and two optional top-level arrays: `relations` (`adaptation-of`, `contained-in`) and `gaps`. Work ids with no provider are `manual:<slug>-<year>`, with `providers: {}`. A work keeps the year its note gives; a second year for the same title stops the build. The slug drops apostrophes and accents. `tests/unit/fixtures.test.ts` now checks all fixture files together: no id in two files, and every relation endpoint, shelf entry and gap resolves to an item in some file. The builder stops, naming the fixture, raw file, record and line, on an unreadable note line, a repeated line, or a work or collection dated two ways. `npm test` is 115 tests in 17 files (74 in 14 before). No package added. Found by the Raccoon and fixed: a repeated note line made a duplicate relation, a work dated two ways became two works, accented titles lost their letters in the id, and the cross-file shelf check could not go red.
