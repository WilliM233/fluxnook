# 1.6.2: The sheet and the stars

The Journal on a work's page has buttons now.

- Log a read (a watch, on a film or show) from the heading: say where it stands, add dates, stars and a first entry pinned to a page, chapter, issue or episode if you like, then Log it.
- Click a card's stars to rate it. The left half of a star is the half; with a star focused, the arrow keys move a half step.
- Edit a read to change its dates, status or stars, or Delete it. The delete says how many entries go with it.
- Add an entry under any read, and Edit or Delete any entry. The time on an entry can be set back.
- Start a second Reading and the sheet says "You're already reading this. Finish or abandon that read first." and stays open.

**Under the floorboards.** No new route, migration or dependency; every sheet posts to the `/api/v1/occurrences` routes from 1.6.1. `journal-section.tsx` is now the server half (it computes every string) and `journal-cards.tsx` the client half that owns the cards and sheets. The sheets live under `src/app/(app)/journal` so 1.6.3's shelf offer mounts the same ones. `src/components/ui/star-control.tsx` is the control. `src/lib/journal/forms.ts` turns a sheet's fields into the route bodies and gives the box's local today and now; a Reading read sends `finishedOn: null`, never a missing key. A read's `PATCH` answer and the item page's read now carry `updatedAt`, and the card shows whichever of its own answer and the page's read is newer (Rummage R1). `scripts/browse/1.6.2.sh` is the Browse: 84 checks, 0 failed in the Binder's run. `npm test` is 782 tests and `npm run test:e2e` 176 of 176 after the Raccoon's fixes. `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.6.2`. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: a card showing stars the heading beside it disagreed with after a change in another tab; a star clicked on a read deleted in another tab leaving a ghost card; a delete of something already gone staying open on "Try again"; a place typed with no words silently dropped; an arrow pressed during a save lost; an entry's stored time five minutes off the one the sheet showed. It also found that an overlay click or Escape threw typed words away, now guarded. The full e2e run before those fixes had one red, `search-comics.spec.ts` line 368, a Postgres deadlock in the harness's `resetComics` against another worker's import, with 11 tests not run after it; the spec passed alone on the rerun and the report is kept whole.

**Not yet.** Started shows no "A date, or leave it" hint, because a date field can't hold one in Chromium; the drawing has it. The card's stars are 18px in small targets, as drawn, not 44px. The card's stars have no Clear; Edit this read does. The Journal page, the read count and mean on a shelf row, the Done offer on a shelf, and the chapter's end-to-end Browse are 1.6.3's. The Written field and the page's entry date are in two time zones that agree on this box and in CI only, until the hosted deploy in Volume 2. An expired session on a sheet says "Sign in to see your journal." and keeps what you typed; signing in again from the sheet is Volume 2's.
