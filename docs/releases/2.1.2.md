# 2.1.2: The shell and the builds

The app on the phone is still the stub: a page that keeps shelves and nothing else. The real nook comes with Chapter 2.2.

- Fluxnook now installs on an Android phone as its own app, from an apk built on every pull request.
- It installs on Windows too, from a `-setup.exe`, with its shelves kept apart from the app.
- Add a shelf, swipe the app away, open it again: the shelf is there. A new build installed over the old one keeps it.
- The page says which build you are looking at, under the version.
- An older build can't be installed over a newer one, on the phone or on Windows.

**Under the floorboards.** `src-tauri/` is new: Tauri 2.12.5 around `device/dist`, identifier `app.fluxnook`, `useHttpsScheme: false` written out and pinned by `tests/unit/shell-config.test.ts`. The scheme was decided on a probe (`device/probe/`, `probe.yml`) read in both WebViews: every row came out the same under `http` and `https`, so the tie went to Tauri's default. `navigator.locks` and PGlite work in both WebViews, so 2.1.1's lock guards a second window on Windows. A Content-Security-Policy in `tauri.conf.json` (`script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'`, no `'unsafe-eval'`) is served by the preview too, so the e2e runs under the shell's policy. `.github/workflows/build.yml` builds the signed apk (`ubuntu-24.04`) and the installer (`windows-2025`), 4 m 41 s and 5 m 11 s cold against a 30-minute cap; it attaches both to a tag's Release when there is one. The apk is signed with one keystore L holds (certificate SHA-256 in the README); `scripts/version-code.mjs` makes the `versionCode` rise with the version, so Android refuses a downgrade, and `allowDowngrades: false` does the same on Windows. The Raccoon found the apk linked at 4 KB page alignment, which a 16 KB-page Android can't load; `src-tauri/build.rs` now links it at 16 KB and the build reads the library's alignment itself (`scripts/elf-load-align.mjs`). The page pads for the status bar (`viewport-fit=cover`, safe-area insets), since Android 15 and later draw the app edge to edge. `package.json` is `0.2.0-ch.1.2`. Nothing under `src/` or `supabase/` changed. `scripts/browse/2.1.2.sh` is the Browse.

**Not yet.** The real nook on the phone: Chapter 2.2.
