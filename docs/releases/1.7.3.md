# 1.7.3: Collection and Shopping

You can see everything you hold in one place, and everything your shelves are still asking for.

- Collection is in the header, after Journal. It lists every copy you hold, newest first, 50 a page, with a line for the printing ("Hardcover · covers 8 issues", "Paperback · Anchor Books · 2011") and your condition, date and note under it. Edit and Remove are on the row.
- The lede counts the copies and the works they make yours, and the works you own through a collected edition: "5 copies of 5 works, newest first. 38 more works are yours through what they collect."
- A chip rail filters the rows by Books, Comics, Films or TV. The counts above it don't move.
- Shopping, beside it, lists the works on your shelves you hold no copy of, shelf by shelf, in the nook's order. A work on two shelves is listed once. A backup story is left out and counted, since you'd buy the comic that prints it.
- I own this on a Shopping row opens Own a copy. Save it and the row leaves the list.
- Each shelf's card in the nook now ends in "· 38 owned" when you hold some of its works.

**Under the floorboards.** No migration, no dependency, no route. `src/lib/collection/collection-page.ts` and `shopping-page.ts` are the two reads; `shopping.ts` is the pure grouping (`groupShopping`, `parseType`). `toFind` in `owned.ts` is the one fragment for "neither held nor via, not a backup", used by Shopping and by Collection's button, and it uses `is distinct from 'backup'` because a film's form is null and `<>` drops every film. The Journal's cursor is now `{ at, id }` so both pages share it; the URL is unchanged. `NOOK_ORDER` is exported from `nook-page.ts` and Shopping groups by it. `loadNook` returns each shelf's `owned`, passing `e.item` into `heldBy` and `viaBy`, and `countsLine` takes it. `OwnCopySheet` holds while a write is out (Cancel disabled, Escape ignored) and tells `onClose` whether a post was tried; its two new doors, a Collection row and a Shopping row, read the page again only after one was. `search.tsx` replaces the URL only when it differs, since a replace on a remount could land after the next Back. `vitest.db.config.ts` sets `maxWorkers: 8`: a worker per core on a 32-core box ran Postgres out of slots (`53300`). `scripts/browse/1.7.3.sh` is the chapter's exit Browse; the Binder's run reads 299 checks, 0 failed, on live Comic Vine and Open Library, and L walks it once by hand. `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.7.3`. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: Cancel closing the Own a copy sheet over a write in flight, which overwrote a kept note and let a second Add make a second copy; "Every work on your shelves is yours" printing on shelves where it wasn't so; a shelf you hold all of, through a backup's host, never saying so; a long unbroken title or shelf name pushing a phone sideways; Escape dropping the keyboard to the top of Shopping.

**Not yet.** Every Shopping row carries its work's printings for a sheet it may never open; the page was 936,101 bytes on a production build, and it waits for a warm hit past 1 s on one (about 0.07 s today, about 1.17 s on `next dev`). An answer lost after Add it landed can still be followed by a second Add, which makes a second copy; it waits for an idempotency key or the next change to the copies route. `/collection` reads the whole catalog for its "more works" count and waits with the lookup-by-item index for the same trigger. A printing made by a tick or Own a copy stays in the catalog after its copy is removed, so an item page can list a Format row nobody holds; L chose that at this chapter's Q1, and it waits for the item-edit Deferral or for L finding the rows noise at the Browse. Shopping has no paging (the 1.5 paging Deferral covers it). Copies are yours alone until Volume 2.
