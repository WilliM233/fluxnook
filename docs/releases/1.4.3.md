# 1.4.3: What this hardcover collects

A collected edition can say which issues it holds now, and every issue says where it has been collected.

- On a hardcover, trade paperback or omnibus, Edit contents opens a list of every comic in your catalog, grouped by series, with a filter for a series or a title. Tick what the book collects and Save contents.
- The edition's page then reads "Contents: Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (Mirage) Vol. 1 #1 to #7, Raphael: Me, Myself and I". Gaps read as they are: "#12, #14 to #15, #17, #19 to #21".
- An issue's page says which editions collect it: "Hardcover · with #2 to #7, and Raphael: Me, Myself and I". Its series name is a link, and the Series line gives its place in the run with links to the previous and next issue.
- A series page has a Collected in section under its issues, one row per edition holding any of the run.
- A comic from Comic Vine says so under its cover and in its sources, with a link back.

**Under the floorboards.** New route `PUT /api/v1/editions/<id>/contents` (see `docs/API.md`): up to 500 uuids, each once, every one a comic that is not the edition's own item; the rows are replaced in one transaction under a lock on the edition, so a bad id writes nothing. A comic's edition only, else 409. `src/lib/catalog/contents-line.ts` is the one formatter for the Contents line and both Collected in rows, free of the database so the picker prints it too. `src/lib/catalog/contents.ts` is the write. `item-page.ts` gains the series fact, the collected-in editions and `loadPickerComics`, run only for a collected comic; `series-page.ts` gains `collectedIn`. The picker, `contents-editor.tsx`, gets its list as page props and sends nothing but the Save. A Save keeps the place of every comic the edition already held and appends new ticks in list order. A blank series text counts as no series. No migration, no package, no provider call. `npm test` is 541 tests (510 at 1.4.2), `test:db` 173 (152), e2e 84 listed (72). `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.4.3`. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: an unattached sibling folded into the wrong run on an issue page, a Save that reshuffled a printing, a lapsed session answering "Try again" forever, the same uuid in two cases passing "each once", and a blank series label printing as a bare number. `docs/STACK.md`'s Comic Vine row now names the stub's `--dir`, its 500 and its `missing-` covers.

**Not yet.** "Owned via" on an issue's Collected in line waits for the collection chapter. The picker's pretick from a title stem, a Creators fact, and the picker at phone width are not built. Edit contents appears only on a collected comic's edition, though the route takes any comic's. A cover past 5 MB is still dropped, not fetched smaller. The picker lists all comics and filters in the browser; a catalog past a few thousand needs a fetch. Series text from a Different import and from hand entry are two groups until the merge lands.
