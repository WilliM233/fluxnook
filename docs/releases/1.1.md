# 1.1: The shelves get their wood

Still nothing new on screen. The nook now has somewhere to put things: every table the whole plan needs, filled with the three fixtures for one test user.

- Films, books and comics each have a place, with the details that belong to each (a book's author, a comic's series and number).
- Shelves keep sections and ordered entries. A work can sit on a shelf once.
- A read or watch is logged on its own, with half-star ratings from 0.5 to 5, and can carry journal entries.
- Copies you own are kept apart from reads. A collected edition holds the issues inside it, and owning it is how you own them.
- Everything you keep is private until you say otherwise.
- Deleting an account deletes everything it kept and leaves the shared catalog alone.

**Under the floorboards.** 17 new tables (11 catalog, 6 content) in `src/db/schema.ts`, 18 in `public` with `profiles`. Four migrations: `catalog`, `content`, `updated_at` (custom: `public.set_updated_at()` and 17 triggers) and `items_form_required`, each opening with its Down. Every enumeration is `text` plus a named check. `visibility` is on `shelves`, `shelf_entries`, `occurrences`, `journal_entries` and `copies`, default `private`; `owner` is on `shelves`, `occurrences` and `copies`, the rest reach it through a parent. `shelf_entries (section, shelf)` is a composite key onto `shelf_sections (id, shelf)`, so an entry can't sit in another shelf's section. `src/db/client.ts` is the Drizzle runtime client; `postgres` moved to dependencies. `npm run fixtures:load -- --user <email>` (`src/fixtures/load.ts`, run through `tsx`, new devDependency, already in the tree under drizzle-kit) loads the catalog by upsert on source key and a user's content only when they have none; `--replace` clears it first. After a reset and one load: 454 items, 125 relations, 37 edition contents, 398 shelf entries, 26 occurrences, 5 copies. `npm test` is 190 tests (183 before), `npm run test:db` 34 (2 before). The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: a book or comic with no `form` passed its check because a null check passes; two loads at once wrote one user's content twice; a fixture naming an unknown edition died with a raw Drizzle error; an email in different case found no user. `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.1`. `docs/STACK.md` has the `postgres` and `tsx` rows. No page, route, or `config.toml` setting changed.
