# 1.7.1: Copies

You can say what you own.

- Every item page has an Editions section now, films and shows included. Own a copy opens a sheet: pick a printing the catalog knows, or a format it doesn't (a Blu-ray of a film, a hardcover of a novel), then a condition, the day you got it and a note if you like.
- A printing you hold shows a line per copy: "Your copy", "Good · since 12 March 2019", your note. Edit changes the condition, the day or the note; Remove lets the copy go and keeps the printing in the catalog.
- A printing you hold nothing of ends in I own this, one click, no sheet.
- A comic you own through a collected edition says so under its facts ("Owned via Ultimate Collection 1 (Hardcover)"), and that edition's row in Collected in ends in "Your copy". A backup story says "Yours via <the comic it is printed in>".
