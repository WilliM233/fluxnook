# 1.4.1: Comic series, found

You can search for a comic series now, and the nook tells you if you already keep it.

- Search has a Comics chip. Pick it and only Comic Vine is asked; pick All and films and shows, books and comic series come back in three labelled groups. If one source is down, the other two still answer.
- Each series row shows its cover, name, start year, publisher and issue count, with a Series pill.
- Each row says whether it is in your catalog, looks like one you have, or is new. A hand-entered run is matched on its name, its first year and, if you wrote one in brackets, its publisher: "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (Mirage) Vol. 1" looks like the 1984 Mirage series, and never like a series from another publisher. Your collected editions are matched too.
- The group says what Add will bring once it lands. A footer line now credits Comic Vine on every page.
- Comic search needs a Comic Vine key. Without one the group says "Comic search isn't set up on this nook yet" and nothing else is affected.

**Under the floorboards.** Two appended migrations, `20261029182922_series` and `20261029182929_series_updated_at`, each with its Down in the header: tables `series` and `series_sources` (shaped as `franchises` and `franchise_sources`, no `visibility`) and a nullable `item_comics.series_id`, restrict on delete. The `series` text column stays for hand-entered runs. Nothing writes a `series` row yet. `src/lib/providers/comicvine.ts` is the client: a 20 s timeout, no redirects, a `User-Agent` carrying the version and `COMICVINE_CONTACT`, HTTP 420 or 429 or a velocity error in the envelope as `ProviderBusy`, and no thrown value or log line holding the URL, since the key rides the query string. `ProviderError` and `ProviderNotConfigured` take a provider name, and `ProviderBusy` is new, all in `src/lib/providers/errors.ts`. `seriesStatuses` and `sameSeries` in `src/lib/catalog/match.ts` are the series rule, a sibling of `catalogStatuses` because a series group has none of an item's fields; `normalizeSeries`, `seriesPublisher` and `collectedStem` are the folds in `normalize.ts`. New route: `GET /api/v1/providers/comicvine/search` (see `docs/API.md`). Tests run on nine recordings in `tests/fixtures/comicvine` served by a stub on 4012 (`npm run comicvine:stub`; `comicvine:record` re-records); CI needs no secret. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: a search listing one volume twice gave two rows with one key, a label in Comic Vine's "(1984)" form never matched, the five collected editions could be led by a one-edition group with a shorter title, and a one-issue group read "1 issues". `npm test` is 472 tests (323 at 1.3), `test:db` 117 (94), e2e 61 (51). `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.4.1`. No package was added.
