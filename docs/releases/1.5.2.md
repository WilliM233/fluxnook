# 1.5.2: The shelf in hand

A shelf is something you can work on now.

- Add works from your catalog: find by title, author or series, pick a section to put them in, tick as many as you like. Works already on the shelf show as ticked, with their number.
- Drag a work by its grip to move it, within a section, into another, or above the first one. A finger works (hold a moment, so scrolling stays scrolling). So does the keyboard: focus the grip, Space, the arrows, Space again. Escape puts it back.
- Mark each work Unmarked, Done or Skipped. Done here means past it on this list, not read or watched; the journal keeps that. The counts follow.
- New section, Rename, Delete section (its works move above the first section, none are lost), Remove, Edit shelf and Delete shelf (it asks once, and the works stay in the catalog).

**Under the floorboards.** No route, no migration. Every click posts a 1.5.1 body to `/api/v1`. The shelf page is one client view (`shelves/[id]/shelf-view.tsx`) seeded from the server's read; a status applies the route's answer, a move shows its numbers at once and reads the shelf again when it lands, and every other write ends in `router.refresh()`. New dependencies: `@dnd-kit/core` 6.3.1 and `@dnd-kit/sortable` 10.0.0, exact, with `@dnd-kit/utilities`, `@dnd-kit/accessibility` and `tslib` arriving beside them (`docs/STACK.md`). The grip is the only part of a row that starts a drag. One `SortableContext` runs over the works and the section headings in display order, so one arrow is one place and crossing a heading moves the work into that section; where a drop lands is `src/lib/shelves/drop.ts`, pure and unit-tested, and the optimistic numbers come from `order.ts`, the server's own rule. A drop from below onto a heading lands at the end of the section above it, which departs from the plan's "top of that section" (plan D2). `loadShelfPage` is one statement, so one snapshot. `loadPickerWorks` (`src/lib/catalog/picker-works.ts`) rides the page's props: nothing is fetched when the sheet opens. `coverPublicUrl` moved to `src/lib/cover-url.ts` because the row now renders in the browser. `scripts/browse/1.5.2.sh` reports to `browse-results/`, and a failed precondition ends its run. `npm test` is 655 tests (637 at 1.5.1). `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.5.2`. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: a status marked while a move was out reading Unmarked again when it landed or was refused, a work removed meanwhile coming back as a ghost row, a stale second tab showing a work under the wrong section, and the Add works test unable to tell list order from tick order.

**Not yet.** The Add to shelf sheet on the item page, its chips as links, Add the run on the series page, the import sheet's line and `GET /api/v1/shelves` are 1.5.3's, with the chapter's end-to-end Browse. The shelf page's payload is heavy: the TMNT page measured 1,241,994 bytes warm, mostly its 326 rows carried twice, and the picker's list is about 94 kB of it; the plan's Deferrals hold the trigger for a read route and for paging, and the Nookkeeper's call on that is open. The pointer drag has no test of its own beyond one at row 300 of the TMNT shelf; the walk is L's on the Browse. A refused status or Remove on a long shelf says so in one line under the list, far from the row. Sharing a shelf by link waits for Volume 2.
