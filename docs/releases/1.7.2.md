# 1.7.2: Ticks and marks

You can tick off what you own without leaving the page you are on.

- A comic series page has a box under every issue. Tick it and that issue is a floppy you own: "Owned · Floppy". Untick it and it lets go. Tick twice from two tabs and it reads "Owned · 2 floppies".
- An issue a collection you hold already covers says so ("via Ultimate Collection 1"), and "also via" once you tick the floppy too. The heading counts it all: "you hold 33, 33 of them via a collection".
- Unticking a floppy that has a note or a condition asks first ("Let go of this floppy?"). One with nothing on it just goes.
- On a shelf, a work you own says "owned" under its title, and one you own through a collection says "via" and the collection's name.
- On the ISBN card there is an "and I own it" box. Tick it, Add this edition, and the printing and your copy go in together. A printing you already hold says "In your collection".

**Under the floorboards.** No migration, no dependency, no route change. `loadSeriesPage` takes the caller and returns `form`, `held`, `via` and `floppies` per issue and `heldIn` for the page. `firstViaTitle` in `owned.ts` is a new fragment (`own_*` aliases) tested to agree with `viaBy` and `loadOwnership`; `loadShelfPage` uses it for the row's mark. `src/lib/collection/ticks.ts` is the pure rule (`newestFloppy`, `asksFirst`, `takesFloppy`). The tick is `issue-tick.tsx`: server-rendered disabled and enabled on mount, a ref guard on a controlled input, the box holding its own count until its refresh lands. An untick reads `GET /api/v1/copies?item=` and then deletes, so the rule runs on the copy as it stands; a note saved in the milliseconds between the two is still deleted (a Deferral). The card asks the same `GET` in `lookUp`, and `addEdition` follows the import's 2xx with the copy for both the card and the match sheet, behind a ref guard. A refused or lost answer makes the page read again before the box takes another click. `ConfirmSheet` stays open while its call is out: Cancel is disabled and Escape is ignored until the answer. `tests/reset-comicvine.ts` takes the copies and source-less printings a tick leaves on an attached issue, and `holdBrowseVolumes` in `tests/db/harness.ts` serialises the two files that hold the Browse volumes. `scripts/browse/1.7.2.sh`: 112 checks, 0 failed in the Binder's run, and it runs the two tick reds (A14) through Playwright partway. `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.7.2`. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: Cancel past a slow Remove closing the sheet over a delete in flight; a lost answer leaving an empty box over a real copy; a card whose A4 read lost its connection vanishing; Add again after a lost answer making a second copy (the box's Add now asks what you hold first, whenever the printing was already in the catalog); Back from the box's Add showing the shelf without its mark; "also via" appearing on the click instead of the answer.

**Not yet.** `/collection`, `/collection/shopping`, the header's Collection link and the nook card's count are 1.7.3's, with the chapter's exit Browse. An issue held only as digital shows an empty "Owned" box (the heading counts it); that waits for the digital Deferral. An untick leaves its floppy printing in the catalog, so the item page can list a Floppy row nobody holds; it waits for the item-edit Deferral. Every answered tick refreshes the whole series page, which waits for a run past 200 issues. The warm hit on the TMNT shelf reads in two modes on `next dev`, about 0.17 s and about 0.42 s; the Browse's five hits ran 0.178 s to 0.452 s, and the index Deferral fires at 0.5 s.
