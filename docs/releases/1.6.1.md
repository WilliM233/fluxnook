# 1.6.1: One open read

The item page has a Journal now, and reads can be logged behind it.

- A work's page shows your reads of it under Journal: how many, the stars of the last one you finished, and whether you're on one now.
- Each read is a card: Reading or Done or Abandoned, when you started and finished, five stars, and the entries you wrote along the way, newest first, each pinned to a page, chapter, issue or episode when you said which.
- A work you haven't read says "Nothing logged yet. Log a read and the journal starts here." A film or show says "watch".
- Your 26 Done reads from the fixtures show as Done cards with no dates and unrated stars.
- You can be on one read of a work at a time. A second one says "You're already reading this. Finish or abandon that read first."

**Under the floorboards.** Six route handlers under `/api/v1/occurrences` (read create, update and delete; entry create, update and delete), all in `docs/API.md` with what someone would get wrong. A `POST` writes a read and its first entry in one transaction. One migration, `occurrences_one_open`, a partial unique index on `(owner, item)` where the status is `in_progress`; its Down is in the file, and the lib turns its `23505` into the 409, so two Readings at once end with one. `src/lib/journal` holds the writes (`reads.ts`, `entries.ts`), the item page's one query (`item-reads.ts`) and the pure parts with no database import: `words.ts` (the verb and every label per type), `anchor.ts`, `stars.ts`, `lines.ts`, `order.ts` (the card order, so the page and 1.6.3's shelf row read one rule). `loadItemPage` gains `reads` as its eighth read. `src/components/ui/stars.tsx` is the star, drawn as SVG; 1.6.2 builds the control on it. Another person's read or entry is 404, never 403, proved with two local accounts in `tests/db/journal-owner.test.ts`. On the item page On your shelves now follows Journal. `scripts/browse/1.6.1.sh` is the Browse: 103 checks, 0 failed in the Binder's run. `npm test` is 755 tests, `npm run test:e2e` lists 151, and `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.6.1`. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: a year 0 date or a timestamp zone past 15:59 answering 500, a `null` date refused on a `POST` that a `PATCH` takes, an entry posted under a read being deleted answering 500, and an entry of zero-width characters landing as a blank card. One full e2e run had one red, 1.5.2's pointer drag in `shelf-rummage.spec.ts`, row 301 where 302 was expected; it passed alone on the rerun and the report is kept whole.

**Not yet.** Nothing in the Journal is a button. Log a read, the star control, Edit on a read, Add an entry and Edit and Delete on an entry are 1.6.2's, so for now those writes are the routes only. The Journal page, the read count and the mean on a shelf row, and the chapter's end-to-end Browse are 1.6.3's. A fixture load with `--replace` now deletes a person's entries with their reads, and the count it prints doesn't name them; the Raccoon's note N4 in the plan names the trigger. Dates print in the server's time zone until the hosted deploy in Volume 2.
