# 1.3: Books come to the nook

You can search for a book now, or type the number off its back.

- Search has a Books chip. Pick it and only Open Library is asked; pick All and films, shows and books come back in two labelled groups. If one source is down, the other still answers.
- Each book says whether it is already in your catalog, looks like one you have, or is new. Books are matched on title and author, never year, because Open Library's years are often off. The cover comes with an add.
- Type or paste an ISBN, hyphens and all, and the box shows that one edition: publisher, year, pages, format, and whether its book is already yours. A mistyped digit is caught before anything is sent.
- Add an edition and the book goes into the catalog if it isn't there, with the printing recorded under it. The Stephen King books you loaded can take Open Library's details instead of being duplicated.
- A book's page lists its Editions. The TMNT collected editions show theirs too.
- No key is needed. Book search works on a fresh clone.

**Under the floorboards.** One generated migration, `20261029004338_editions_publisher_pages`: `editions` gains `publisher` and `pages` (a check keeps `pages` above 0), appended, with its Down in the header. An ISBN is an `edition_sources` row with source `isbn` and the ISBN-13 as key; Open Library ids are `works/OL…W` on `item_sources` and `books/OL…M` on `edition_sources`. `src/lib/providers/openlibrary.ts` is the client (20 s timeout, a `User-Agent` carrying the version and `OPENLIBRARY_CONTACT`, redirects followed only under `OPENLIBRARY_BASE_URL`); `ProviderError` moved to `src/lib/providers/errors.ts` and both clients share it. `src/lib/isbn.ts` normalizes and checks the digit for the browser and the routes. `catalogStatuses` takes a rule per type, so books compare author where films compare year. New routes: `GET /api/v1/providers/openlibrary/search`, `GET /api/v1/providers/openlibrary/isbn/<isbn>`, `POST /api/v1/editions/import`; `POST /api/v1/items/import` takes `source: "openlibrary"` (see `docs/API.md`). Covers go to `covers/openlibrary/<id>.jpg`. Tests run on recordings in `tests/fixtures/openlibrary` served by a stub (`npm run openlibrary:stub`, `openlibrary:record` re-records); CI needs no secret and starts both stubs in Playwright's global setup. The Raccoon found, and the chapter fixed: an ISBN with a stray `%` threw a 500, a page count past a Postgres `integer` broke an import, a multi-author work missed itself when its names came back in another order, two Sames at once failed the second with a 409, and a printing of a book already in the catalog kept "'Salem's Lot" as a title of its own because of the apostrophe. `npm test` is 323 tests locally (243 before), `test:db` 94 (66), e2e 51 (33). `package.json` is `0.1.0-ch.3`. No package was added.
