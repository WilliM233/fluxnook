# 0.6: The Mirage run, on the shelf

Still nothing new on screen. The last Volume 0 chapter puts L's TMNT Mirage comics tab into the nook's test data, including which issues sit in a hardcover on the shelf.

- All 326 rows of the Mirage tab are comics now: issues, one-shots, the Micro and Mini series, the backup stories, the crossovers and the movie tie-ins.
- The five Ultimate Collections are works in their own right, each with a hardcover edition and a copy. The 37 issues they collect are owned through them.
- The 47 backup stories each know which comic they were printed in. Two hosts are Volume 1 issues the tab lists by name; the other 35 are listed as anthologies, even the seven that are tab rows under another name, until the comics provider can match them.
- The 26 issues L has read are logged as reads, on their own. Owning a book never counts as reading it.
- One shelf, "TMNT, the Mirage run", keeps the tab's 14 sections in order.
- Nine crossover rows have no date in the tab. They are written down as gaps, not guessed at.

**Under the floorboards.** `npm run fixtures:build` now writes `supabase/seed/fixtures/tmnt-mirage.json` from `supabase/seed/raw/google-sheets/tmnt-mirage.csv`, through `scripts/fixtures/mirage-tab.mjs`, which reads each section by its own rule. `king-films.json` and `king-books.json` are byte for byte unchanged. `src/fixtures/shape.ts` gains a `comic` item (`form`, `series`, `series_kind`, `number`, `month`, `source_date`; `year` may be null here only) and the top-level `editions`, `copies` and `occurrences`, all read as empty when absent. A shelf has `entries` or `sections`, and its `url` is optional. The shape now also refuses a relation from an id to itself, the same relation twice, and an item id that starts `edition:`. The build writes nothing until every fixture has built (`scripts/fixtures/build.mjs`), refuses two sources with one output file, and its log line counts every non-empty array. `slug()` moved to `scripts/fixtures/slug.mjs`; `exportOf()` and the new `sheetOf()` are in `tests/unit/fixtures/helpers.ts`. `tests/unit/fixtures.test.ts` checks edition contents, copies, occurrences and edition ids across all files. `npm test` is 183 tests in 21 files (115 in 17 before). The numbers in the spec differ from the build in three places: 14 sections, not 13; 9 gaps, not 47 (the slid-left sections date from `Name`); 253 `issue` items, not 229. Found by the Raccoon and fixed: a section header named `constructor` or `toString` passed as known, a Crossover row could silently fork an id from another section, an anthology with an undated backup stopped the build or went unrecorded, a repeated header made two sections with one name, two sources could share an output file, and an item id could pass as an edition id. No package added.
