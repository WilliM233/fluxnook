# 0.2: The empty nook

The nook has a front door. It only opens on your own machine for now, but it opens.

- Four commands bring Fluxnook up at http://localhost:3000. The README lists them.
- Sign in with just your email. No password, anywhere. A link arrives in the local mailbox; one click and you're in.
- Asking for a link with a new address makes your account. There's nothing to fill in.
- Your nook knows who you are and says, honestly, that it's empty.
- Sign out and the door shuts behind you, on every device you were signed in on.
- An expired or used link tells you so and lets you send another.

**Under the floorboards.** Next.js 16.4.2 App Router with every package pinned exactly (`.npmrc` `save-exact=true`, reasons in `docs/STACK.md`). Supabase local through the pinned CLI, Mailpit as the mail catcher. Auth is PKCE magic link through four route handlers under `/api/v1` (`docs/API.md`); `src/proxy.ts` refreshes the session. One app table, `profiles`, filled by a trigger on `auth.users` insert; drizzle-kit generates into `supabase/migrations`, the Supabase CLI applies, and `.claude/skills/migrations/SKILL.md` says how. RLS is off, so the Data API is kept off app tables: `public` is out of `[api] schemas` and `auto_expose_new_tables = false`. The magic link takes JSON only, closing a login CSRF the Raccoon found. 21 Vitest and 11 Playwright tests; the Rummage's seven lines each have a test file, seen red before green. `package.json` is `0.0.0-ch.2`.

**Not yet.** CI, Playwright reading the mailbox, a database test for the signup trigger, and the seed fixtures: Volume 0 Chapter 3.
